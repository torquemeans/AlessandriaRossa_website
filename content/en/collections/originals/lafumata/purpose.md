## 'La Fumata', or, 'The Puff of Smoke'

To begin, our nature is that of an amorphus, organic working group. The ever-expanding fluidity of our organisation continues on to acquire more or less individuals who contribute to the whole of our body of work as needed. Of course, our body of work consists of writings and translations rather than action as of now. The puff of smoke only ever appears after the rifle has fired, but nevertheless, it precedes the next shot. To arm the real movement, truly revolutionary doctrine remains the proper weapon. As we collect these doctrines, dogmas, and catechisms, we expand our knowledge and learning, which is the true and hidden purpose of our group. Due to this, our articles are not translated by 'artificial intelligence' or translation applications.

## The future of our work

As of now, our primary task is the translation of the 'Teleriunione' articles by Quinterna. This is of a temporary nature, to an extent, and other translations will follow, particularly of the magazine *n+1*. Along with this, we plan to write articles of our own on specific issues. This, however, will wait until we acquire a much greater degree of education on revolutionary doctrine and theory, to avoid making drastic errors. There is no "membership count" of our group, as it is organic, but there are a number of people involved in our work. Expansions are not exactly sought out but we do not mind such a thing. If you look at the top of the page, you will see a nonfunctional "Search" button. This is because we are currently devising such a system, and integrating it. Among things we are considering is a set of videos to upload to this site and others, however this has not been determined at this point.

## Our place

We exist in a time of brewing crisis. The bourgeoisie continue to expropriate and expel working people from owning any semblance of property. More are pushed to buying and not possessing, and transition to the great mass of people without reserves. However, we have not yet seen a truly revolutionary crisis. But it seems that one will occur within the coming decades. Our place is to prepare for such a crisis become educated enough to engage with the abolition of class society.
